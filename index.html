Business Analyst · case study

eMAR Implementation & Process Improvement

Amberleigh Care Home (ACH)

Process improvement

Illustrative summary diagram of the approach.

Problem & objective

Examine the care home's existing medication administration process and identify opportunities to improve documentation, workflow, accuracy and efficiency through an electronic Medication Administration Record (eMAR) system.

My contribution

Participated in analysing the existing workflow and in developing proposed improvements. My documented contributions:

  • Developed and examined the AS-IS process flowchart
  • Carried out the Ishikawa (fishbone) root-cause analysis
  • Contributed to the team-developed TO-BE process map
  • Identified process-improvement opportunities through digital medication management

Methods & tools

  • Business process mapping
  • AS-IS / TO-BE analysis
  • Ishikawa diagram
  • Root-cause analysis
  • Gap analysis
  • eMAR implementation considerations

Outcome & learning

Produced process analysis documentation showing current workflow challenges and where digital medication management could improve administration. Improvements are proposals; implementation outcomes have not been measured.

Key lesson: involve care professionals when planning technology implementation.

Data Analyst · Excel capstone project

Pharmaceutical Sales & Performance Analysis

ApexPharma Logistics Ltd.

Goal

ApexPharma distributes medicines and healthcare products across four commercial regions. Inconsistent distributor, sales-representative and regional records made it hard to see the key sales drivers. The aim was to clean and analyse the data and build an interactive management dashboard for product demand, regional, distributor and sales-team analysis.

My role

Data Analyst, working in Microsoft Excel on a dataset of 350 transactions and 14 fields: identified and fixed inconsistencies, removed duplicates, trimmed spaces, standardised categories and regions, checked missing values and formats, built PivotTables, charts and a slicer-driven dashboard, and wrote business recommendations.

Tools & approach

  • Microsoft Excel
  • Power Query
  • TRIM / Find & Replace
  • Duplicate removal
  • Text to Columns
  • PivotTables
  • Slicers
  • Interactive dashboard
  • Exploratory data analysis

Outcome & learning

$169.4mTotal revenue ($169,410,900)
44,168Units sold
350Transactions
≈ 41%Antimalarials share ($69,086,700)
  • Antibiotics were the second-leading category ($39,236,400).
  • Top products: Artesunate 60mg Injection (≈ $38.93m), Artemether/Lumefantrine 80/480mg (≈ $30.15m) and Ciprofloxacin 500mg (≈ $21.37m) — together ≈ 53.4% of revenue.

Strengthened my skills in data cleaning, PivotTable analysis, dashboard development and turning raw records into actionable commercial insight.

My career began in civil and structural engineering, where I built extensive experience in quality control, inspection, project coordination and professional leadership — first with Julius Berger Nigeria Plc, then in the Inspectorate and Quality Control function of the Lagos State Government. I also led the Lagos Chapter of the Association of Professional Women Engineers of Nigeria (APWEN) as Chairman from 2021 to 2023.

After moving to the United Kingdom, I worked in healthcare and care services. Seeing how much safe, person-centred care depends on efficient systems, accurate records and clear processes inspired my move into HealthTech business analysis and data analytics.

Through 10Alytics and Amdari I have built practical project skills in business analysis, healthcare business cases, stakeholder management, process improvement, Excel, Power BI and SQL, and I am developing my Python.

My long-term aim is to help develop and implement sustainable, data-driven digital health solutions that improve healthcare delivery, operational efficiency and patient experience.
